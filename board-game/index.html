Board game

Stay Awake

A print-and-play board game set in the world of The Fragmented Collector. One of you isn't a captive. Roll through the house that doesn't breathe, crawl through the vents, and find the Collector before you are collected.

Players
4–8
Time
30–45 min
Ages
14+
Price
Free
Download the gameRead the rules
1

A secret role

Everyone gets a role card face down. One card only says The Collector. Everyone else is a Captive.

2

Roll through the house

Like snakes and ladders: crawl through the vents to get ahead, and slide back if you doze off.

3

Two-sided cards

Every card has a text for Captives and a darker one only the Collector may use. You say what you do, never what the card says.

4

Night and talk

At night, all eyes close and everyone taps the table while the Collector sedates someone. Then you talk, accuse and vote.

Two texts, one card

The white half is for Captives. The dark half is only for the Collector, who may also use the white half to blend in. Every text grows out of the book and the song COLLECT HER.

Print at 100% (actual size), not “fit to page”, or the cards come out too small. Thick paper works best for the board and the figures.

You also need 1 die, scissors and glue or tape, 8 paper clips and a phone as a 1-minute timer.

The game has dark themes from the book, like control and sedation. Nobody is hurt on the table: being “collected” just means you're out of the game.
