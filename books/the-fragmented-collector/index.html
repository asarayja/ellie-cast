The Fragmented Collector

Ellie Cast

Trenton Makes, The Collector Sedates but The Protector is always watching.

One Brother conspires to collect the memories of his past to redesign, rebuild and reclaim identity, his autonomy. - While the other brother collects for power, control, “love” and “healing.”

Standing in the middle of it all is The Healer.
She never asked for this role but in some strange way, fate chose this destiny for her. Now she must navigate the murky waters of dysfunctional family dynamics, deities and traumas in order to escape total isolation. The Collector’s playground. This home is not where the heart is, it’s where the void beats, breathes and isolates all living things. A place where the walls are soundproof, Gates and windows secured with steel structures at the touch of a button. Even if somehow a collection manages to wake, the rest of the world will never hear them screaming.

But does anyone ever truly escape The Collector?
For The Healer’s sake, let us pray that not a fragment of his tainted soul can bleed through cracks and ink into her light.

The rules are simple: Stay aware. Stay away. Stay awake.
In order to remain without sedation,The Healer must choose:

Fall in or Fall out, Decide.
