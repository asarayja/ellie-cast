About Ellie

Ellie Cast

Ellie Cast has written stories for as long as Ellie can remember – first in notebooks with a pencil, later late at night at the kitchen table.

The books are often about people at a crossroads, and about the small choices that change everything. Rainy days, strong tea and books that make you lose track of time are among the favourites.
